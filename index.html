Eco Pocketbook

An offline field companion for Queensland ecologists.

What's in the app

  • RE Guide: search Queensland regional ecosystems by number, bioregion, land zone or keyword, with HVR and remnant thresholds.
  • Land Zone Key: work through diagnostic questions to identify a site's land zone.
  • Tree Cover Tool: record line-intercept cover by species and strata.
  • Botanical Glossary: plain-English plant terms for field identification.
  • Geological Glossary: rock, landform, weathering and soil terms, plus the 12 Queensland land zones explained.

Everything works without a phone signal.
