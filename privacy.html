Eco Pocketbook

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Eco Pocketbook is an offline field reference for Queensland ecologists. This policy explains what happens to information in the app. In short: nothing you enter leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the internet at all once installed.

Information stored on your device

To be useful in the field, the app saves a few things on your phone so they're still there next time you open it:

  • Tree Cover Tool entries (species, strata and measurements you record)
  • Your favourite regional ecosystems
  • Your progress through the Land Zone Key
  • The screen you last had open

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location, camera and other permissions

The app does not ask for or use your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone or any other device permission.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
